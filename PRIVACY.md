# Money Sim privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026. Draft: have it checked by someone qualified before you publish the app._

Money Sim is a money-education app. All money in it is virtual.

## What we collect

**Nothing leaves your device.** Money Sim has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.

The app stores these on your device only:

- the name and date of birth you enter at sign-up (the date of birth decides which features suit your age);
- your answers to the starting quiz, your budget plan and the spending you log;
- your lesson progress, XP, stars, badges, mascot and wardrobe choices;
- your virtual portfolio and trading-simulator history.

## Backup codes

If you tap **Copy backup code**, the app copies your saved data to your device's clipboard so you can keep it or move it to a new device. Anyone who has your backup code can see that data, so keep it private. The app only reads a backup code when you paste one in and tap **Restore**.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. No server is involved. You can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

## Children

Money Sim can be used by children with a parent's or guardian's permission. Users under 18 do not see the trading simulator. Because no data leaves the device, we do not collect personal information from children.

## Deleting your data

Tap **Erase all progress** on the Profile tab, or delete the app. Both remove everything the app stored.
